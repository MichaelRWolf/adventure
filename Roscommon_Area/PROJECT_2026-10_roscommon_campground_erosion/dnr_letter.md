# DNR Letter

To: Jason Lewicki <lewickij@michigan.gov>

Subject: Volunteer erosion control -- gully draining to The Cut, south of Roscommon

Mr. Lewicki,

I regularly visit the open space on The Cut at the south terminus of Everett Road, south of Roscommon -- Momma Nature at her best.

A gully along the access two-track drains road runoff into The Cut, and it has reached a tipping point:

- **Decades:** eroded sand has built a wide delta, much of it submerged in the stream.
- **Overall:** the bank has retreated about 30 feet from the original embankment.
- **Years to decades:** the gully itself has cut about 15 feet.
- **Last 1-2 years:** headcuts have reached the road bed.
- **Now:** vehicles have only just begun detouring uphill around the damage. That will accelerate erosion dramatically and soon make the road or adjacent camping spots unusable.

Photos attached (see Attachments).

Your resources are surely stretched across bigger projects, so I'm happy to pitch in on this small (to you) one. I have ideas for a dam or silt catcher but know just enough to be dangerous. I want to avoid a dam that is undercut by a plunge pool, flanked by poor bank attachment, or overtopped -- any of which would ACCELERATE (not retard) the headcuts.

Can you confirm this is your unit's land, and whether you'd support my volunteer-amateur best-effort, suggest a better approach, or require a permit given the proximity to the river?

Best,
Michael Wolf

P.S.  Thanks for all you (and the other DNR staff) do to make nature be a resource for everyone!  It makes a huge difference to the quality of life here.

# DNR Letter

To: Jason Lewicki <lewickij@michigan.gov>

Subject: Volunteer erosion control -- gully draining to The Cut, south of Roscommon

Mr. Lewicki,

I regularly visit the open space on The Cut at the south terminus of Everett Road, south of Roscommon.
It is a beautiful spot to enjoy Momma Nature at her best.

A gully along the access two-track drains road runoff directly into The Cut, and it has reached a tipping point.
The headcuts have advanced into the road bed.
Vehicles are now accelerating the erosion and detouring further uphill, which spreads the damage.
Left alone, it will soon make either the road or the adjacent camping spots unusable.
Photos attached.

I expect your resources are stretched across much bigger projects, so I'm happy to pitch in on this small (to you) one.
I have some ideas for a dam or silt catcher, but only know enough to be dangerous.
I want to be sure a dam is not undercut by a plunge pool, flanked by bad attachment, or overtopped at the weir in a way that creates a plunge pool and further ACCELERATES (instead of retards) the headcuts.

Can you confirm this is your unit's land, and whether you'd support my volunteer-amateur best-effort, suggest a better approach, or require a permit given the proximity to the river?

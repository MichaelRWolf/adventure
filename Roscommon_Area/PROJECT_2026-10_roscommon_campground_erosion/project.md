# Roscommon Campground Erosion

Volunteer erosion control for a gully cutting into the access road at a free
state-land site on The Cut, south of Roscommon, MI.

## Location

- Open space on The Cut at the south terminus of Everett Road, south of Roscommon
- Coordinates: 44.4281, -84.6536 (Plus Code C8HW+6HW)
- Gerrish Township, Roscommon County, MI 48653
- Likely state forest compartment along the Cut River (Sections 35-36, T24N-R3W) -- unconfirmed

## Problem

- Clean sand gully drains road runoff directly into The Cut
- Headcuts have advanced into the road bed
- Vehicles accelerate erosion and detour further uphill, spreading damage
- Near tipping point: road or adjacent camping spots soon unusable

## Proposed approach (pending DNR response)

1. Treat headcuts first: lay back vertical faces to ~2:1, cover with brush, butt-ends uphill, staked
2. 3-5 low brush check dams below: 6-12 in tall, keyed 6+ in into bed and banks, center notch lower than ends
3. Every crest below road edge (prevents flanking into ruts)
4. Armor each spillway landing (brush mat or rock) against plunge-pool scour
5. Space by elevation: base of upper dam ~ crest of lower dam
6. Real fix: water bar / diagonal dip at top of slope to shed road runoff onto flat vegetated ground

## Risks

- Plunge pool undercutting a dam
- Flanking from poor bank attachment
- Overtopping that accelerates headcuts instead of retarding them

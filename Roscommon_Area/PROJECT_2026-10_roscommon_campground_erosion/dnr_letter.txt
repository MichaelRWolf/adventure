Re: Volunteer erosion control -- gully draining to The Cut, south of Roscommon

Dear Mr. Lewicki:

I regularly visit the open space on The Cut at the south terminus of Everett Road, south of Roscommon -- Momma Nature at her best.

A gully along the access two-track drains road runoff into The Cut, and it has reached a tipping point:

  - Many years: eroded sand has built a wide delta, much of it submerged in the stream. The bank has retreated about 30 feet from the original embankment.
  - Last 1-2 years: headcuts have reached the road bed.
  - Now: vehicles have only just begun detouring uphill around the damage. That will accelerate erosion dramatically and soon make the road or adjacent camping spots unusable.

Photos attached (see Attachments).

Your resources are surely stretched across bigger projects, so I'm happy to pitch in on this small (to you) one. I have ideas for a dam or silt catcher but know just enough to see that a dam could be undercut by a plunge pool, flanked by poor bank attachment, or overtopped -- any of which would ACCELERATE (not retard) the headcuts.

Please confirm that this is your unit's land, and whether you'd support my volunteer-amateur best-effort, or whether you'd suggest a better approach.

If this letter has reached the wrong person, I would appreciate your pointing me to whoever handles this area. If you are able to forward it, please CC me so I can follow up directly with them.

Sincerely,

Michael Wolf

P.S.  Thanks for all you (and the other DNR staff) do to make nature a resource for everyone!  It makes a huge difference to the quality of life here.

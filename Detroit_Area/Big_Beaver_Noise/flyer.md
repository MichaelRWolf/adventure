# Rooftop Noise From 999 W. Big Beaver Rd

Constant mechanical noise from rooftop units at 999 W. Big Beaver Rd (Troy, MI) carries many blocks into apartment courtyards, back yards, and residential streets. It sounds like a lawn mower or leaf blower running all day. On residential side streets, it is the loudest noise you hear. It should not be detectable at all in a home or on a residential street. It has gone on for about three years.

## What we are asking

- The property owner and manager identify the rooftop equipment responsible.
- They measure the noise from neighboring properties, not just from the roof.
- They reduce it. Common fixes: variable-speed fan controls, sound enclosures or barriers, vibration isolation, and repair.

## Why it matters

Troy City Code Chapter 88, Sec. 9.1 defines anything that "annoys, injures, or endangers the safety, health, comfort or repose of the public" as a public nuisance.

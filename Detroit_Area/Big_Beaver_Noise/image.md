# Poster Art

Use for the top of `poster.md`. Generate, save as `poster_art.png` (or similar), then add the link here and in `poster.md`.

## Recommended tool

- **ChatGPT (image generation):** good enough for this. The poster's words stay in text, so the image needs no lettering.
- **Midjourney:** pick this if the ChatGPT result looks generic and you want a stronger illustrated style.
- Model versions change. Confirm the current model in the tool you use.

## Prompt

```text
Bold flat editorial illustration, high contrast, limited palette of slate gray, deep teal, warm cream, and one strong red for sound. Landscape 3:2. No text, no logos, no watermark.

Left third: a large commercial building seen from the street, with two rooftop HVAC units humming. Thin, clean red concentric sound waves radiate out from the units, fading as they travel across a tree-lined residential street.

Center: a row of quiet houses with soft afternoon light, leafy trees, a parked car, and a small courtyard between apartment buildings. The sound waves reach the edge of the frame but do not settle here.

Right third, foreground: a warm cream living room visible through a window. In the room, a person sits on a sofa with both hands pressed firmly over their ears, eyes closed, face calm but strained. A single lamp glows. Simple, uncluttered shapes, no fine detail.

Mood: calm home under siege. Readable at small size, works in black and white for photocopying.
```

## Variants

- **Simpler:** remove the courtyard and the parked car. Keep the building, the waves, and the person.
- **Black and white:** replace the palette line with "monochrome, black ink on white, bold shapes, no gray gradients."

## Problem

- Continuous mechanical noise from rooftop units at 999 W. Big Beaver Rd (Troy, MI 48084).
- Sounds like a lawn mower, leaf blower, or helicopter from above.
- Audible well beyond the site: apartment courtyards and residential back yards and streets, at least 3/4 mile away.
- Not noticeable on Big Beaver or Crooks, where traffic masks it.
- Ongoing for about 3 years.
- Cranes on site (2026-10-23) for a lift. Operators say the work is lifting, not the roof units. Security did not know of the crane.
- The noise is mechanical equipment, not construction. Construction-hour rules don't apply to it.
- Residents inside the building are shielded from the noise, so they may not hear it. The people hearing it are outside.
